What is Yoga?

Yoga is a practice with roots in South Asia. It can bring together physical postures, breathing exercises, and meditation. People practice in different ways and for different reasons.

What Does Yoga Include?

  • Poses, also called asanas
  • Breathing exercises
  • Meditation and focus

Why Do People Practice Yoga?

Regular practice may help develop flexibility, balance, and body awareness. Choose a level and pace that feel comfortable for you.
